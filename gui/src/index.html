Folding Everywhere — pure-Rust fp32 protein design

Project ↗ Author ↗
Three protein models in one program. No Python, no PyTorch, no GPU — pure Rust on the CPU. Nothing leaves this machine; the server listens on 127.0.0.1 only.
Fold a protein sequence to 3D all-atom coordinates.
Model
ESMFold2 is stochastic: same seed → identical structure (bit-exact to a PyTorch fp32 run at that seed). ESMFold1 has no seed. Loops (trunk refinement) and sampling steps (diffusion) default to the official release depth (20 / 68); lower them for a faster, lower-quality fold. Our benchmarks use 3 / 14 to make the bit-exact fp32 check fast. (A single diffusion sample is produced.)

Ligands
Load a structure to see its ligands.
Only the ligands present in your structure are shown: the coordinates come from your file, and this program supplies the bond orders and aromaticity, which a PDB cannot express. 56 ligands ship with it, matched to your file by atom name, so they work on any structure.
Design
The contig is a comma list: a number is that many designed residues, A106-106 keeps motif residue 106 of chain A. 10,A106-106,10 builds 10 new residues, the catalytic residue, then 10 more. Same seed → identical design, byte for byte. On 4 CPU cores the bundled example takes ~1.7 min at T = 2 and ~70 min at T = 100.
