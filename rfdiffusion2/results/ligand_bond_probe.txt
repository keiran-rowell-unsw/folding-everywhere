CONCLUSION
For the primary demo inputs OpenBabel is doing BOTH jobs from 3D coordinates:
  1. ConnectTheDots      - connectivity from interatomic distance + covalent radii
  2. PerceiveBondOrders  - hybridisation, ring perception, aromaticity, kekulisation
Only PH2 supplies CONECT records.

This is materially larger than "port a subset of perception rules": aromaticity
perception and kekulisation are thousands of lines of heuristics, and their
output feeds bond_feats -> bond_emb directly, so the orders (not just the
connectivity) change the network input.
